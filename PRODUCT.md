# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are potential employers, clients, collaborators, and people interested in Abhishek's work and interests. They explore the site to understand his range, evaluate concrete work, find ways to start a conversation, and follow ongoing work.

## Product Purpose

Abhishek's personal website is both a creative portfolio and a personal digital home. It should make his multidisciplinary curiosity tangible, provide paths to real projects and skills, support contact or collaboration, and connect visitors to writing, experiments, social profiles, or other ongoing work.

Success means visitors understand Abhishek's range, can evaluate his real work, and can continue the relationship through contact or followed channels.

## Positioning

The interactive curiosity house is the site's signature mechanism and core identity: Abhishek's interests and creative range are expressed as an explorable inhabited world rather than a conventional portfolio index.

## Operating Context

The current experience is a full-screen interactive 3D house containing spaces associated with study, cooking, music production, gaming, and observation. Visitors view and gently orbit the scene in a web browser.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the interactive curiosity house as the signature centerpiece.
- Preserve the name Abhishek; other positioning and content may evolve.
- Future portfolio, biography, contact, writing, experiment, and social content remains undecided until supplied or confirmed.
- Do not fabricate projects, clients, testimonials, metrics, credentials, or other proof.
- Current implementation uses TanStack Start, React, Three.js, React Three Fiber, Tailwind CSS, and Cloudflare deployment tooling.
- The 3D experience uses demand rendering and includes scene loading, failure fallback, semantic scene description, and reduced-motion handling.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Abhishek.
- Signature asset and identity: the interactive curiosity house.
- Existing title and concept language includes “A little world of curiosity.”

## Product Principles

1. Make multidisciplinary curiosity explorable rather than merely claimed.
2. Let the curiosity house remain the memorable center of the experience.
3. Connect expression to real evidence so visitors can evaluate actual work.
4. Offer clear paths from discovery to contact and continued following.
5. Use only supplied or confirmed personal and professional claims.
